01 — What I do

Everything on your social media, handled

Every plan is built from the same six pieces — you pick the platforms and the pace, I do the rest. Nothing here is subcontracted, templated or generated.

Content calendar & strategy

A month mapped out before it starts: what posts on which day, tied to your promotions, your season and your quiet weeks. You approve one calendar — then you’re done deciding for the month.

Posts & captions

Custom graphics and photos, with captions and hashtags written for your business — not a template. Scheduled and published at the right hours, so you never touch the composer.

Reels & short video

Shot on location, cut vertical, subtitled and covered with a clean thumbnail. Reels that show the actual work, in your actual voice — the content that reaches past your followers.

Stories & highlights

Weekly Stories with polls, countdowns and Q&A stickers, plus highlight covers that keep your best moments findable. This is where a local business looks alive day to day.

Community — replies

Comments and DMs answered in your voice, on a set schedule. A reply within hours is the difference between a booked job and somebody messaging the next business on the list.

Boosted posts & ads

Recommendations on which posts are worth money, and how much. The budget goes from your account straight to Meta or TikTok — never through me. Full management comes as the ads add-on.

No stock photos, ever. Every image on your feed is shot by me, a working photographer — the same hands that plan your posts hold the camera. See what that looks like on the photography portfolio. khrystyna-donik-photography.netlify.app →

03 — Process

A month in four moves

The same rhythm every month, so nothing on your feed depends on you remembering anything.

  1. 01

    Listen & audit

    A 30-minute call and a scroll through your current page: what worked, what died, and who your audience actually is.

  2. 02

    Plan the month

    You get a calendar — posts, Reels, Stories, promo dates. You approve it in one message. No weekly approvals.

  3. 03

    Shoot & create

    I shoot on location, cut the Reels, write the captions and hashtags. Photos from your own sessions — never stock.

  4. 04

    Publish & report

    Everything goes up on schedule, and at month’s end you get a plain-English report with the numbers and next month’s moves.

Your whole time commitment: one 30-minute call, and one message a month approving the calendar. Everything else is mine.

06 — FAQ

Questions I get asked first

Do you post every day?

Not usually — daily posting is how feeds burn out. What every plan guarantees is cadence: never a week of silence, with posts spread to match your audience’s habits. Momentum and Always On include Stories on open days, which is where the daily presence really lives.

Who takes the photos and the video?

I do. I’m a working photographer — the photos and the vertical video come from your own sessions and your own location, so nothing on your feed is a stock image three competitors are using too. See the photography work.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Every plan starts with a two-month minimum, because momentum needs at least that long to show. After that it’s month to month, and you can change plans or stop any time — the content calendar, the captions and the photos you’ve already paid for stay yours.

Do you guarantee followers or sales?

No honest manager can. What I guarantee is the work: the cadence, the quality and the reporting. Followers and bookings follow consistency — that’s what the monthly reports track, and what next month’s plan is adjusted from.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram and Facebook on every plan, plus a monthly Google Business Profile post. TikTok is included in Always On, and paid ads on any platform come through the ads add-on.

What do you need from me to start?

A 30-minute call, access to the page, and any photos you already love. After that you approve one content calendar a month — that’s the whole time commitment on your side.
